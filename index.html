Services & transparent pricing

Explore the therapies offered by 5 Elements. Some modalities are selectively combined during an appointment rather than booked separately. Listed prices and appointment times are from the clinic's published website; confirm current availability when scheduling.

Acupuncture

$11060 minutes

Fine, sterile needles are placed at selected points as part of an individualized acupuncture session. The practitioner assesses your concerns and may incorporate complementary techniques where appropriate.

Often sought for musculoskeletal pain, headaches, stress-related concerns, and general wellness. Results vary and no outcome is guaranteed.

Shockwave Therapy

$15560 minutes

Targeted acoustic pressure waves are applied to specific areas as part of a noninvasive treatment approach for certain muscle, tendon, and soft-tissue complaints.

The clinic lists heel, knee, shoulder, hip, back and other localized pain concerns. A consultation helps determine suitability.

Sound Chamber Therapy

$13590–120 minutes

A longer wellness session featuring sound-wave exposure combined with color-based and bio-geometric frequency concepts, as described by the clinic.

This is a complementary wellness modality; evidence for specific medical claims is limited. Ask about the session experience and intended goals.

Salt Therapy (Halotherapy)

$2515 minutes

A short session involving exposure to finely dispersed dry salt particles in a controlled setting, intended as a relaxation and wellness experience.

Not a replacement for treatment of asthma, COPD, infections, or allergies. Consult your clinician if you have respiratory conditions.

Aqua-Chi Foot Bath

$13560 minutes

A seated foot-bath experience in which an electrical device operates in the water while your feet are immersed.

The clinic markets this as Aqua-Chi therapy. Claims of removing toxins or changing blood pH are not established medical benefits.

Cupping Therapy

Used with other therapiesTime varies

Suction cups are applied to targeted areas of the skin. Cupping may be incorporated into an individualized session for temporary muscle tension or discomfort.

Separate price and duration are not published. Temporary circular marks and skin sensitivity may occur.

PEMF Therapy

Price on requestTime varies

Pulsed electromagnetic field equipment delivers changing magnetic fields to selected areas. The clinic lists this as one of its available treatment technologies.

Ask about equipment, suitability, and how it fits into your session. Certain implanted electronic devices may be a contraindication.

Near-Infrared & Light Therapy

Price on requestTime varies

Selected areas are exposed to red, blue, or near-infrared wavelengths of light. Treatment choice depends on the intended wellness or tissue-care goal.

The clinic does not publish a standalone rate or duration. Eye protection and device-specific precautions may be necessary.

Microcurrent Therapy

Price on requestTime varies

Very low-level electrical currents are applied to selected tissues as part of a personalized therapeutic session.

Ask the practitioner whether this is included in a broader treatment. Suitability depends on your medical history and implanted devices.

Low-Level Laser Therapy

Price on requestTime varies

Low-intensity light is directed at specific areas without cutting or heating tissue in the manner of a surgical laser. Sometimes called photobiomodulation.

The clinic lists it for localized pain and tissue-related concerns. Treatment duration and pricing are not published.

Pricing note: “Price on request” does not mean the therapy is necessarily sold separately. The clinic says techniques may be selectively combined based on individual needs. Please confirm pricing, session length, and current offerings before booking.

Veterans caring for veterans

Veteran-owned. Veteran-focused.

Owner and primary practitioner Samantha Lin is a U.S. Navy veteran. Her firsthand understanding of military service informs the clinic's commitment to helping fellow veterans navigate their care options. 5 Elements identifies itself as an approved VA Community Care provider for acupuncture and massage therapy; this website focuses on acupuncture and the other listed wellness services, not full-body massage.

How to request VA Community Care

1Contact your VA care team

Speak with your VA primary care provider about whether a referral is appropriate.

2Request 5 Elements

Tell your care team you would like to receive eligible services at this clinic.

3Share clinic details

Provide NPI 1033727631 to help identify the provider.

4Confirm authorization

After VA approval and clinic receipt of the referral, coordinate scheduling with 5 Elements.

VA Community Care contact listed by the clinic: (918) 252-8000. Eligibility, covered services, and authorization must be confirmed with the VA. An approved referral is required; a request alone does not guarantee coverage.

Meet your practitioner

Samantha Lin

Owner • U.S. Navy veteran • Acupuncture practitioner

Samantha served in the U.S. Navy in San Diego and Pearl Harbor before training in massage therapy in Honolulu and earning a master's degree in acupuncture and oriental medicine from the Tai Hsuan Foundation. Her professional experience includes work in Japan and Dubai. She established 5 Elements in Bartlesville in 2001.

Before your visit

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a referral to book acupuncture?

Private-pay visitors should contact the clinic about scheduling. VA Community Care treatment requires an appropriate VA referral and authorization.

Can I book cupping, PEMF, or light therapy on their own?

The clinic says treatment methods are selectively combined according to individual needs. Ask whether the modality you want is available separately and what it would cost.

Does the clinic provide full-body massage?

Full-body massage is not offered on this website. The clinic's older website notes that massage was not accepting new clients. Contact the clinic about currently authorized VA services.

Are the published prices guaranteed?

No. The listed amounts are taken from the existing website and should be confirmed directly with the clinic before your appointment.

Can these services replace medical treatment?

No. Complementary wellness therapies should not replace diagnosis or evidence-based care for medical conditions. Discuss any health concerns or contraindications with your treating clinician.
